About the festival

STAR HOUR IS MORE THAN A FESTIVAL: IT IS A SPACE WHERE YOU ARE SEEN.

It is a gathering point for experts from different countries of Europe and the world. France, the USA, Germany, Ireland, Spain, Brazil — we bring strong people together.

“Star Hour” is a meeting space for people who feel that the time has come to show up, reveal their talents, widen their horizons and create a life that truly suits them.

Everyone has their own path and their own light.

Here you do not need to become someone else to be noticed. Here you can get to know yourself more deeply, present your ideas, meet like-minded people and discover new opportunities.

We believe that true transformation begins within, but unfolds in contact with the world. Sometimes a single meeting, one conversation or one bold step becomes the start of a completely new chapter.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the festival for?

The festival suits people interested in self-knowledge, personal and spiritual growth, psychology, mindfulness, creativity and new forms of interaction. The programme brings together practices and meetings for participants with different life and professional backgrounds.

Do I need experience in personal growth practices?

No, unless a specific session has special requirements. You can come with an interest in the topic, a personal question or a wish to explore new approaches. The description of each practice will help you choose the right format.

What will I get from taking part?

You will be able to try different practices, hear experts, talk with other participants and spend time on your own development. Everyone's experience will be individual: a lot depends on the sessions you choose, your interests and what you bring to the festival.

Can I become a festival expert?

Yes. We review applications from specialists whose topics fit the philosophy of “Star Hour”. Tell us about your expertise, suggest a topic and state your preferred participation format.

Can I present my product or brand?

A separate participation format will be offered for original products, creative projects and brands, once confirmed by the organisers. The terms for display, promotion and sales will be set out in the corresponding partner offer.

How do I register?

Choose the ticket or participation format that suits you and fill in the registration form. After you submit your application, you will receive information about next steps and organisational details.
