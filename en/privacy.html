Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect on the luxembourgfestival.com website (the international “Star Hour” festival, Luxembourg), why we do so and what rights you have. We process data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the organising team of the “Star Hour” festival. For any questions about personal data, write to info@luxembourgfestival.com.

2. What data we collect

Trainer application form (the “Become a trainer” form):

  • first and last name;
  • city and country of residence;
  • phone number (WhatsApp / Telegram);
  • Instagram link (optional);
  • specialisation / product.

Payment for participation and tickets: payment is made on the secure page of the Stripe payment service. We do not receive or store your bank card details or other payment details. We only receive the information needed to confirm payment (for example, name, email address, amount and payment status).

Tickets and event entry: after a ticket is paid for, we create an electronic ticket and store in a database: the buyer's name, email address, the unique ticket number (also encoded in the QR code), the Stripe payment identifier, and the date and time the ticket was created and sent. If you bought several tickets, each one carries the buyer's name. At the festival entrance a team member scans the ticket's QR code; we record that the ticket has been used and the time of entry. No photos or videos are recorded during scanning.

Email: if you write to us at info@luxembourgfestival.com, we receive your address and the content of your message.

3. Why we use the data and on what basis

  • Reviewing your application and contacting you — to contact you about taking part in the festival (your consent and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract).
  • Registering participation, payment and issuing tickets — performance of a contract and compliance with legal obligations (accounting).
  • Sending the ticket by email and entry control (checking the ticket's authenticity, protection against reuse and forgery, counting guests) — performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in organising the event safely.
  • Answering your questions — our legitimate interest in communicating with visitors to the site.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Who we share data with

  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) — payment processing, including by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Revolut Pay. Stripe processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • Website hosting provider (Aruba) — hosting of the site and the ticket database, and email processing (including sending tickets).
  • Cloudflare (the Turnstile bot-protection service for forms) — when you submit a form, your browser sends Cloudflare technical data (for example, the IP address) to check that a human is filling in the form.
  • Members of the organising team who need this data to handle your application.
  • Public authorities — only if required by law.

5. Third-party services on the site

To display the site, Google Fonts are loaded, and the OpenStreetMap map is loaded in the geography section. When these elements load, your browser sends the services technical data, such as the IP address. By default the site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we enable visit statistics (Google Analytics) or advertising tools (Meta Pixel — Facebook and Instagram), they will be enabled only after your consent in the cookie window. You can decline or change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page. In that case, visit data (IP address, device type, pages viewed) may be sent to Google and Meta; their privacy policies apply separately.

6. How long we keep data

We keep data no longer than necessary for the purposes stated above or as required by law. Application data for which no participation decision was made is deleted at your request. Ticket data (name, email address, ticket number, entry record) is kept for no more than 12 months after the festival date, after which it is deleted or anonymised; payment information that we are legally required to keep (accounting) is kept for the periods set by law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy of it;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data or restriction of its processing;
  • object to processing and request data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal).

To exercise your rights, write to info@luxembourgfestival.com. We will reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Luxembourg this is the National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD), cnpd.public.lu.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data against loss, unauthorised access and disclosure: the connection to the site is secure (HTTPS), only the organising team has password-protected access to the ticket list and scanning, and Stripe messages are verified by digital signature.

9. Changes to the policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, and the date of the last update is shown at the top.
