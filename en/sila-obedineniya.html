The route of your star hour

Five hours.
From “I'm ready” to “we're doing it”.

Timing is given from the start of the programme. Inside: theory, practices, introductions, content and work on joint projects.

00:00 — 00:2020 minutes
Opening · shared practice

01. Step into your star hour

The three hosts open the day with a shared story of unity. Through a gentle practice of attention and movement, we restore contact with ourselves. In pairs we say: “What did I come with? What is my strength? What am I ready for?”

Result: a personal intention for the day and a first introduction without formal business cards.

00:20 — 01:0040 minutes
Live conversation · theory · work in trios

02. Scale begins with permission

A short conversation between the three hosts: how to find partners, combine audiences and agree on contributions. Then the practice “My scale — 2027”: we choose a big goal and turn it into a clear result. In trios we explore what support is needed for the next step.

Result: one goal for 2027, your own zone of strength and a request to future partners.

01:00 — 01:4545 minutes
Visibility · practice · feedback

03. I am seen. I am heard.

We build a self-presentation: “I help… My strength is… Right now I'm looking for… I can offer…”. We practise it in pairs, receive caring feedback and try again. We learn to invite collaboration clearly and confidently.

Result: a 45-second self-presentation and a concrete proposal for collaboration.

01:45 — 02:0015 minutes
Break

Coffee, fresh air and a conversation you want to continue

Time to rest and save the contacts of those with whom a connection has already appeared.

02:00 — 02:4545 minutes
Guided networking

04. Your people recognise you

Three rounds of introductions in changing small groups: “What I create”, “What I can give”, “Who and what I'm looking for”. On a shared map of offers and requests we find matches. We choose the people we want to keep working with.

Result: a map of useful connections and mutual interest from which a partnership can start.

02:45 — 03:4055 minutes
Collaboration lab · teams of 3–5 people

05. Create what did not yet exist

We gather around a shared idea: a joint live broadcast, a meeting, a game, an intensive, a trip or another project. On one sheet we record the audience, the benefit, the format, each person's contribution, the way to promote it and a first trial launch. The hosts help refine the idea and the agreements.

Result: a draft of a real collaboration with roles, a first step and a date for the next meeting.

03:40 — 04:1535 minutes
Content practice · filming together

06. Show up together — right here

We prepare a short introduction video or an announcement of an idea. In mini-groups we film on our phones: who we are, what unites us and whom we invite. You can choose a photo with text or stay off camera. Publication and tags are optional and by mutual consent.

Result: material for publication and a clear joint call to the audience.

04:15 — 04:4530 minutes
Project gallery · exchange of resources

07. A big idea meets support

Teams place their projects in a shared gallery. Participants get to know them and leave offers of help and contacts, by consent. We look at a few ideas together with the hosts: what to strengthen and how to start with the resources available.

Result: feedback, new points of collaboration and a clarified next step.

04:45 — 05:0015 minutes
Shared finale · personal decision

08. Our star hour begins

Each person records an action for the next 72 hours. Partners confirm the date of the first call and how to stay in touch. We close the day with a shared circle: “What am I taking with me, and what am I ready to create together?”

Result: a personal step, a first agreement and a sense of belonging to a shared movement.

Before the meeting

You can come as yourself.

What if I don't know anyone and find it hard to meet people?

Introductions are built into the programme: there will be questions, small groups and time to settle in. You choose what to share and which activities to take part in.

Do I need a ready project or a large audience?

An idea, experience or a wish to find a direction is enough. For a joint project, different strengths are useful: knowledge, creativity, organisation and the ability to connect people.

What should I bring?

A phone with a charger, a notebook and one idea you want to develop. If you have links to your projects, save them to exchange contacts.

When, where and how much does participation cost?

The second day of the “Star Hour” festival will take place in Luxembourg. The date, address, start time and price will be added once confirmed by the organisers.
